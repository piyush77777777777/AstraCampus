# AI Usage Declaration — AstraCampus (CIT)

*This document complies with Section 6 of the Hackathon Rules and Problem Statement.*

| Model / API | Component | Purpose | Where Prompts are Kept |

## 2. What AI is NOT Allowed to Decide in Our System

> **STRICT COMPLIANCE GUARANTEE:**
> Per hackathon regulations, **no LLM or AI model makes decisions on timetable placement, clash detection, room validity, or booking approvals/rejections.**

1. **Validity & Clash Detection:**
   - All feasibility checks are strictly computed in Python code by `backend/engine/constraints/hard_rules.py` (mirroring the official `validator.py` rules H1 through H14).
2. **Timetable & Exam Generation:**
   - All schedules are solved deterministically using Google OR-Tools CP-SAT (`backend/engine/solver.py` and `backend/engine/exam_engine.py`).
3. **Venue Booking Decisions & Priority:**
   - The booking hierarchy, role category capping, and alternative venue allocation are computed strictly by deterministic priority algorithms in `backend/engine/booking_engine.py`.
4. **Schedule Rescheduling & Disruption Fixes:**
   - Disruption mitigation and minimal-movement recovery are solved by mathematical search in `backend/engine/rescheduler.py` and require explicit human Admin authorization.
5. **AI Proposal Pre-Validation:**
   - Every structured booking output generated by the AI Copilot is pre-validated by Python constraint checkers before being presented to the user or submitted.
